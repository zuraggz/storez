Our story

A small studio with a long memory

Qlonil started in 2014 above a dry cleaner on Oxford Street, with one pattern cutter, a borrowed machine and a stubborn conviction that most clothes were being made too fast to be made well.

A decade later the studio is a little larger and the conviction has not moved. We still cut in small runs, still keep the same three mills on speed dial, and still put every sample through a season of real wear before it reaches the shop.

What changed is who wears it. Pieces that began as a London answer to a London winter now turn up in thirty-one countries, which is a responsibility we take seriously every time we choose a fabric.

How we work

Slower than we could be, on purpose

Every style starts as a paper pattern and ends as a wear test. In between there are usually four samples, two arguments and one fabric we wanted to use but could not source responsibly.

We publish what we learn along the way in the journal — from how to read a fabric label to the arithmetic of buying a boot that can be resoled.
